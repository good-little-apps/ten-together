Support

Learning works best together.

Ten Together is built for a parent and child to use side by side. You read each short line aloud; your child taps a picture or answers out loud; you tap how it went.

Getting started

On first launch the app shows a short welcome with one real lesson page to try, then asks you to add your child and tick anything they can already do. The Today tab then plans a session of about ten minutes.

Daily sessions

Tap Start session on the Today tab. Tap “Got it” only for a quick, confident, correct answer; a hesitant right answer is “Not yet.” Honest taps are what make the pacing right for your child.

A session you leave part-way can be resumed from the Today tab on the same day. Everything answered so far already counts.

When your child gets one wrong

Calmly say the answer, say it together, and move on. The app brings the missed page back later in the session. If your child is frustrated, stop: nothing is lost, and the schedule adapts.

More than one child

Use the child switcher in the Today toolbar to add brothers and sisters. Each child has completely separate progress.

Extra practice

Any unlocked skill can be practiced from the Progress tab, even after the day’s session is done. Practice never changes the review schedule. Long-press a skill to mark it as already known.

Daily reminder

The optional reminder is one notification a day at the time you choose. None is sent on a day you have already finished, and they stop by themselves if you take a week off. Change the time or turn it off in the Guide tab. If the switch has no effect, allow notifications for Ten Together in iOS Settings.

Sound

On letter pages, the speaker button plays the letter’s sound. The chime and counting sounds can be switched off from the menu inside a session.

Your data

Your child’s name, answers and progress are stored only on your device. There is no account and no cloud sync, so deleting the app deletes that data. See the privacy policy for details.

Contact

Something confusing, broken, or wonderful? Use Send feedback in the Guide tab, or email dayton.thorpe@gmail.com. It goes straight to the person who makes the app.
