Ages 2–6 · Reading, math and words

Teach your child yourself, in ten minutes a day.

Most learning apps are built to keep a child busy alone. Ten Together gives you, the parent, a plan for each day, the exact words to say, and what to do when your child gets something wrong. You teach; your child learns beside you.

Side by side

We give you the words. You teach.

1

You read one line

Every page has a short line for you to say aloud, such as “This says /mmm/. What does it say?”

2

Your child answers

They tap a picture, touch objects to count them, trace a letter, or say the answer out loud.

3

You tap how it went

“Got it” or “Not yet.” A missed page comes back later in the session, after you show the answer together.

Real progress, not just play

A plan that follows your child.

  • Reading: rhymes, the sounds in words, letter sounds, tracing and writing letters, sounding out first words, and five little books.
  • Math: shapes, small amounts at a glance, counting, numerals to 10, more and fewer, first sums and taking away.
  • Language: colors, opposites, positions, categories, and words like “same,” “different,” “some,” and “all.”
  • Mastery: a skill counts as learned only after your child gets it right on two different days, and it returns for spaced review so it sticks.
  • Pace: new skills unlock in order, set by your child’s answers, never by age.

Ten Together draws on decades of research into how young children learn, including Siegfried Engelmann’s teaching methods. It is designed for a parent and child to use together, and it is not a replacement for preschool, reading aloud, or play.
