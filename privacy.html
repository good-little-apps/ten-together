Plain-language privacy

Your child’s learning data stays on your device.

Ten Together has no account, no advertising and no tracking. Your child’s name, answers and progress never leave your device. From version 1.3 the app sends anonymous usage counts, which you can turn off. This policy explains exactly what that means.

Effective November 13, 2026

The short version

Everything about your child stays on your device. The app tells us only that something happened, such as “a session was finished,” without saying who did it. We do not sell or share data, show ads, or track you across other apps.

Who we are

Ten Together: Reading & Math (formerly Superior Mind) is provided by Dayton Thorpe (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app or contact us.

Information stored on your device

The app stores each child’s name or nickname, chosen picture, answers, mastery progress and review schedule, along with your settings such as the reminder time. This is kept in the app’s own storage on your device. We cannot access it. There is no account and no cloud synchronization.

Apple device backups may include app data depending on your device and backup settings; Apple controls that service under its own terms and privacy practices.

Anonymous usage counts

From version 1.3, the app sends anonymous usage counts through TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service, so we can see how the app is used and what to improve.

A count records that something happened: the welcome was started or finished, the sample lesson page was tried, a child was added, the starting check was completed, a reminder choice was made, a session was started or finished, or a rating was requested. Some counts carry a coarse number, such as which day of use it is.

With each count, the service receives the app version, operating system version, device model, language and region settings, and an anonymized installation identifier. That identifier is scrambled on your device before it is sent, cannot be used by us to identify you, and is not shared across other apps.

A count never includes a child’s name, age, picture, answers, or which skills they know.

Like any internet request, sending a count necessarily exposes your device’s IP address and standard technical metadata to TelemetryDeck, which processes requests under its own privacy policy. We do not receive IP addresses.

You can turn usage counts off at any time in the app: open the Guide tab and switch off Share anonymous usage under Privacy.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, notifications are generated locally on your device by the app itself. There is no push notification server and no registration with any service. iOS asks for your permission first, and you can revoke it at any time in iOS Settings.

External links and public pages

If you open the research, support, or privacy links in the app, iOS opens the destination outside Ten Together. The destination and your browser receive the technical information normally needed to load a webpage.

These public pages are hosted by GitHub Pages, so GitHub may process requests under its own privacy statement. We do not receive your browsing activity.

Support correspondence

If you choose to email us, we receive the email address, message, attachments, and any other information you include. Feedback sent from the app also includes the app version and device model. We use it only to understand and respond to your request, maintain the app, and meet legal obligations.

We retain support correspondence only as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes and then delete or de-identify it, unless longer retention is legally required.

Advertising and tracking

The app contains no advertising. It does not track you, access the advertising identifier, combine app activity with third-party data, or use data for targeted advertising. We do not sell personal information.

Apple may process App Store downloads, crash reports, or device analytics when you use Apple services or have chosen to share diagnostics with Apple. Apple controls that processing.

Children

Ten Together is designed to be operated by a parent or other adult, teaching a child beside them. Everything the app records about a child stays on the device. The anonymous usage counts contain no information about any child, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can turn anonymous usage counts and the daily reminder on or off in the Guide tab. You can remove a child’s profile in the app, which deletes that child’s learning record.

Deleting the app removes all locally stored learning data, subject to any backup retained under your Apple settings. You may request deletion of support correspondence by emailing us from the address used for the request.

Security

Keeping learning data on-device limits its exposure, but no device or storage system is guaranteed to be secure. Protect your device with an appropriate passcode and keep iOS current.

Changes to this policy

If a future version materially changes the app’s data practices, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure before that version is released. The effective date above shows when this policy was last revised.
